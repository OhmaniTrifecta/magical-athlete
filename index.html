    How to play

    • Each player gets 4 racers. There are 4 races, alternating between the Mild Mile and the Wild Wilds.
    • Before each race, every player secretly picks one racer they haven’t raced yet.
    • On your turn, roll the die and move that many spaces: your main move. Every racer has a power that bends the rules. Tap a card to flip it and read it.
    • The first racer to cross the finish takes the G gold chip, the second takes the S silver chip. Chips get bigger each race.
    • On the Wild Wilds, stars give a B bronze chip, arrows move you, and TRIP! spaces knock you over. A tripped racer skips their next main move to get back up.
    • After 4 races, the player with the most points wins.
